About me

Curious about how children learn, and how to make it joyful.

My work sits where education, storytelling and technology meet. I've spent the last decade building learning content, running operations and listening to parents and teachers, and I still find the questions about how children learn the most exciting part.

Where it started

A classroom, a library and a birthday

I trained as an engineer, but the work I care about most has been with young learners. I completed the Early Childhood Assistant Certificate at the University of British Columbia in 2021, and I spent time in a classroom supporting children aged 5 to 8 with storytelling, phonics and early literacy.

The idea that has stayed with me longest came from home. A visit to a beautiful library changed how my daughter felt about books, and I wanted other children, especially in rural communities, to have that same chance. That became The Library Project.

Storybooks open doors to imagination, curiosity and new possibilities.
