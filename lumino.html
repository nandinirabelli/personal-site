Personal project

Lumino

Telugu language learning for young children, through stories, sound and pictures.

What Lumino is

Lumino is a YouTube channel I co-founded and edited, May 2022 to January 2023. It makes Telugu language learning engaging for young children through short learning videos.

  • Created and produced 26+ learning videos, with narration, sound design and music.
  • Edited and QA-tested audio tracks in GarageBand, Audacity and Adobe Premiere, for clarity, continuity and emotional tone.
  • Designed 50+ alphabet worksheets that pair graphics with phonics cues to reinforce bilingual literacy.

A child learns a language by hearing it clearly, feeling it, and then doing something with it.
