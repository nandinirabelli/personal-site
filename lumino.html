Personal project

Lumino

A zero-to-one children's learning channel: 26+ videos, 50+ worksheets, built by a team of two.

Role
Co-Founder & Chief Editor
Timeline
May 2022 – Jan 2023
Scope
0–1 product, content and production
Focus
Bilingual early literacy

Context

Telugu-speaking families raising children outside India face a specific problem: the child grows up fluent in English and loses the home language. The material that exists is mostly made for children already living in a Telugu-speaking environment, and it does not hold the attention of a child whose default language is English.

Problem

How do you teach a language to a four-year-old who has no practical reason to use it? Direct instruction fails. The content has to be worth watching first and educational second, or it is not watched at all.

Trade-off: production quality against publishing cadence. With a two-person team I chose fewer, better-produced videos over a high-frequency schedule, betting that a child's attention is won by quality of sound and story, not by volume.

Execution

  • Created and produced 26+ learning videos combining narration, sound design and music.
  • Edited and QA-tested every audio track in GarageBand, Audacity and Adobe Premiere for clarity, continuity and emotional tone, including hiss removal and pitch modulation.
  • Designed 50+ alphabet worksheets pairing graphics with phonics cues, so screen time had an offline counterpart.

What I took from it

Audio does more work than video in children's learning content. The episodes that held attention were the ones where the sound design was right, not the ones with the most animation.
