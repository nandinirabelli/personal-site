Operations and analytics at Almabase

Building the dashboards, pipelines and standards a multi-team SaaS company runs on.

Role
Operations Manager & Data Analyst
Timeline
Feb 2016 – Mar 2020
Scope
Cross-team operations and reporting
Focus
Analytics, process design

Context

Almabase is a SaaS company whose teams each had their own way of tracking work. That is normal at a certain size and it stops working at the next one: when every team reports differently, leadership cannot compare anything, and nobody is sure which number is the real one.

Problem

Reporting was inconsistent across teams, which made accuracy hard to guarantee and slowed every decision that needed a cross-team view. The underlying issue was not missing data but missing shared definitions.

Trade-off: standardising naming and process costs every team some local convenience in exchange for company-wide legibility. I pushed for the shared standard, and kept it light enough that teams would actually follow it, since a standard nobody uses is worse than none.

Execution

  • Developed dashboards and process pipelines for SaaS operations, improving reporting accuracy and team efficiency.
  • Created and maintained SOPs, documentation systems and naming conventions so work stayed consistent as teams grew.
  • Managed structured file organisation and version control across multi-team projects.

Impact

4 yrsowning operations and reporting

This page needs your numbers more than the others. Useful ones: how many teams or people used the dashboards, reporting turnaround time before and after, hours saved per reporting cycle, or how many processes you documented. Pick the one you can defend in an interview.

What I took from it

Most "we need better data" problems are definition problems. The dashboard was the easy part; agreeing on what a number meant was the work.
