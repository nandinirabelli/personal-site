My journey

From classrooms to kids' learning products

The path behind my work in education, operations, language learning and early literacy.

  1. Nov 2023 to present
    Masterclass Lead and Product Strategy, Big Life Journal
    • Leading research, content and product strategy for GrowthMinded. See BigLifeJournal.
  2. Jan 2023 to Aug 2023
    Language Translator, Duolingo
    • Translated and QA-tested 30+ Telugu to English learning units. See Duolingo.
  3. Aug 2022 to Mar 2024
    ECE Assistant, Pacific Spirit School
    • Supported classroom teachers for children aged 5 to 8 with storytelling, phonics and early literacy.
  4. May 2022 to Jan 2023
    Co-Founder and Chief Editor, Lumino
    • Produced 26+ Telugu learning videos for young children. See Lumino.
  5. Mar 2020 to Aug 2022
    Homeschooling and early childhood learning
    • Homeschooled my daughter through early childhood, which sparked a deep interest in child-centred, play-based learning. Completed the Early Childhood Assistant Certificate at UBC (2021).
  6. Feb 2016 to Mar 2020
    Operations Manager and Data Analyst, Almabase
    • Built dashboards, process pipelines and SOPs for SaaS operations.
  7. Jan 2014 to Jan 2016
    Teacher and Program Manager, Gyanville Academy
    • Founding team member for a two-year high school program; recruited faculty and co-designed the curriculum.

Along the way I've also built The Library Project, a yearly birthday fundraiser for storybooks, and Book List, an app for parents.
