Career project

GrowthMinded at Big Life Journal

Building an SEL and growth mindset content platform that parents and professionals keep coming back to.

Role
Masterclass Lead & Product Strategy
Timeline
Nov 2023 – present
Scope
Research, content production, retention
Focus
Membership product, SEL content

Context

GrowthMinded is a social-emotional learning and growth mindset platform from Big Life Journal, serving two distinct audiences: parents raising children at home, and professionals (teachers and therapists) using the material in practice. It runs on a membership model, so the product succeeds only when members keep finding value after the first month.

Problem

Two audiences with overlapping but non-identical needs were being served by one content library. Parents want practical help tonight; therapists want material that holds up against an SEL framework in a clinical or classroom setting. Content that splits the difference risks serving neither well, and in a membership business that shows up as churn.

Users and research

I ran user interviews with parents and educators to understand what each group was actually trying to accomplish, and led the research and data strategy that turned those conversations into decisions the content team could act on.

  • Interviewed parents and educators directly rather than relying on support tickets or survey scores alone.
  • Fed the findings into product design decisions and into the membership retention work.
  • Worked with creative teams to check content against SEL frameworks, so professional users could trust it.

Trade-off: aligning every masterclass to a formal SEL framework slows production and constrains what a script can say. I treated that cost as worth paying, because framework alignment is what makes the material usable by the professional segment, and that segment is the harder one to win back once lost.

Execution

  • Managed production of 10+ parenting masterclasses and a set of kids' videos end to end: scripting, audio and video editing, and QA.
  • Owned the content pipeline across creative, editorial and review, so a class moved from idea to publish without stalling between teams.
  • Built the media asset organisation and version control system the team works in, which is what keeps a multi-person content operation from shipping the wrong cut.

Impact

10+masterclasses produced end to end
2audiences served by one content strategy

Add the outcome numbers that make this a PM case study. Strong candidates, if you can pull them: membership retention before and after, masterclass completion rate, member growth over your tenure, or production time per class. One real number with a baseline beats three vague ones.

What I took from it

Interviewing both audiences separately was the decision that mattered most. The parent and the therapist describe the same content in completely different language, and until you hear both, you are guessing at which one the roadmap is actually serving.
