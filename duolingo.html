Career project

Duolingo Telugu–English course

Shipping course content where a mispronounced vowel is the difference between a learner trusting the product and leaving it.

Role
Language Translator & QA (Telugu–English)
Timeline
Jan – Aug 2023
Scope
Individual contributor, course content
Focus
Localization quality, speech QA

Context

Duolingo's Telugu-to-English course teaches English to Telugu speakers. Much of the audio is generated through speech synthesis, which means quality problems are systematic: a single wrong pronunciation rule does not appear once, it appears in every unit that uses it.

Problem

A literal translation can be correct and still be wrong for a learner. Telugu and English differ in word order, formality and sound inventory, so content that passes a dictionary check can still read as stilted, or sound wrong when synthesised. Learners do not file bug reports about this; they quietly lose trust in the course.

Trade-off: the most natural Telugu phrasing is not always the clearest teaching example. For early units I favoured clarity over idiom, so a beginner could map the two languages onto each other, and allowed more natural phrasing as units progressed.

Execution

  • Translated and QA-tested 30+ learning units.
  • Reviewed speech synthesis output for phonological accuracy, correct pronunciation and natural prosody, not just correct words.
  • Documented recurring failure patterns and fed them back as linguistic and technical QA improvements, so fixes applied to the system rather than to one unit at a time.

Impact

30+learning units translated and QA-tested

If you have it: the defect or rework rate on units you reviewed, how many QA issues you escalated that became course-wide fixes, or anything the team tracked on content quality. Even "X recurring pronunciation issues documented" is concrete.

What I took from it

Writing up the pattern behind a bug is worth more than fixing the instance. The feedback that changed how the course handled a sound class outlived every individual correction I made.
